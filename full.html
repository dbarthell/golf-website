Course Notes

  1. Stand behind the ball, pick a target line
  2. Take one practice swing feeling the shot shape
  3. Address the ball, align clubface to intermediate target
  4. One look at the target, then go
  • Rotate like Bryson
  • Stretch w/ arms + hands
  • Right heel + hip initiate
  • Head still
  • Turn head to right at top of swing
  • Shaft straight up at address
  • Head down at top to prevent heel contact
  • Pivot of body
  • Throw w/ right arm to gauge distance
  • Read putt from side; front first feel
  • Speed in bunkers; use full swing
  • Aimpoint from center of cup
  • Accelerate putter like Bernhard
  • Miss to the fat side of the green
  • Par is always a good score
  • Avoid short-sided chips — leave room to work with
  • When in doubt, take one more club
  • Lateral hazard (red stakes): Drop within 2 club-lengths, no nearer the hole — 1 stroke penalty
  • Water hazard (yellow stakes): Drop behind, keeping point of entry between you and the hole — 1 stroke penalty
  • OB (white stakes): Stroke and distance, or local rule drop — 2 stroke penalty
  • Unplayable lie: 3 options, all 1 stroke penalty
